# Boss targets (owner verdicts, 2026-11-05: Look Table rounds 4 and 5)

The owner judged Look Table round 4 (15 act-boss sheets, three per boss) and round 5 (King, Torque and the finale, each sheet generated twice: Codex 0.160 and 0.166, same brief). These sheets are **targets to chase in the engine**, not assets: match silhouette, proportion, palette and the attack vignettes in the game's cutout rigs and print, judged at game zoom.

**Palette rules carry over:** teal only for souls, police blue only for police, blood red only for blood, vermilion only for danger, objectives, Rivet's collar and the King's cape.

## Codex 0.160 against 0.166 (same briefs, same references)
The owner kept four 0.166 sheets (King, Torque, High Command, the scuttler) and two 0.160 sheets (Krump, Root), and dropped both versions of three. Neither version names an image model (both list `gpt-image-2`; the C2PA claim says only "gpt-image"), and the timings were close (152 s against 141 s). **No measurable quality step between the two;** the choice per sheet was about the drawing, not the version.
